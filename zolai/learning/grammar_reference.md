# Zolai AI Grammar Reference

## Critical Rules

### 1. Word Attestation
- NEVER use words not in Bible or dictionary
- If unsure, say "Ka thei kei hi" (I don't know)
- Use word_attestation.py to verify

### 2. "na" Particle
- Possessive: na pa = your father
- Noun maker: lungdam = thank you → lungdam na = gratitude
- When thanking: use "Lungdam!" NOT "Lungdam na!"

### 3. Sentence Structure
- SOV word order (Subject-Object-Verb)
- Ergative "in" marks transitive subjects
- Example: "Pasian in vantung a piangsak hi" (God created heaven)

### 4. ZVS 2018 Forbidden Forms
- pathian → pasian
- ram → gam
- fapa → tapa
- bawipa → topa
- cu/cun → tua
- suah → chuak

### 5. Tense Markers
- -sak: past completed
- -nak: infinitive/purpose
- -ah: present progressive
- -hen: past progressive
- ding: future

### 6. Negation
- lo: general negation
- kei: negative (before verb)
- si: negative imperative

### 7. Questions
- hiam: question marker (at end)
- Example: "Na pai hiam?" (Did you go?)

## Correct Greetings
- Ka dam hi = I am well
- Lungdam = Thank you (NOT "lungdam na")
- Lungdam mahmah = Thank you very much

## Bible as Ground Truth
- Use Bible sentences for correct patterns
- Never guess - use attested sentences
- Pattern types: SOV, quotative, question, negation
